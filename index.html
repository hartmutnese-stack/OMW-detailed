SINGLE-STOCK FRAGILITY CHECK

Measures structural market tension for an equity from price and volume data (no direction, no trading signal).
How it is calculated

Eight factors (0–100, higher = more fragile): Volatility 25%, Upside Extension 15%, Peak Drawdown 15%, Overnight Gaps 10%, Volume Spike 10%, Liquidity 10%, History Depth 15%, Offer Price Distance 15% (optional). Factors without enough data are left out. Young IPOs (< 5 trading days) fall back to 1h intraday candles. Liquidity is converted to USD at approximate rates. Include exchange suffixes for non-US stocks (e.g. RHM.DE for XETRA). All scales and weights are uncalibrated estimates.
